# XPAY Chat User Flows For XPAY AI

## Dang Ky Va Dang Nhap

Nguoi dung tao tai khoan bang so dien thoai va email duy nhat. Mat khau can du manh. OTP email duoc dung de xac minh khi he thong cau hinh. Neu tai khoan cu chua co email, email dung de khoi phuc mat khau co the tro thanh email duy nhat gan voi tai khoan do theo quy tac moi.

## Tin Nhan

Nguoi dung co the:
- Nhan tin voi ban be.
- Gui anh, video va vi tri hien tai.
- Xoa hoi thoai khoi danh sach chat.
- Xoa hoac thu hoi tin nhan bang thao tac giu tin nhan.
- Nhan thong bao tin chua doc.

XPAY AI co the ho tro:
- Tom tat hoi thoai.
- Goi y tra loi.
- Nhan dien viec can lam tu noi dung chat.
- Canh bao khi noi dung chat co rui ro bao mat.

## Ban Be

Nguoi dung co the:
- Ket ban bang so dien thoai.
- Ket ban bang QR.
- Tim ban quanh day khi cho phep vi tri.
- Chan/bo chan ban be.
- Tao link gioi thieu nguoi moi va tich diem.

XPAY AI co the ho tro:
- Goi y cach tim nguoi can lien he.
- Giai thich trang thai ket ban.
- Goi y loi nhan gioi thieu.

## Cuoc Goi

Ung dung ho tro goi thoai va video call. Co lich su cuoc goi, trang thai goi den, goi di, goi nho, may ban va thoi luong.

XPAY AI co the ho tro:
- Tom tat lich su lien lac neu duoc phep.
- Goi y nen goi lai ai truoc khi co cuoc goi nho.

## Nhat Ky

Nguoi dung co the dang trang thai/nhat ky kem anh, voi quyen xem:
- Cong khai.
- Ban be.
- Rieng tu.

XPAY AI co the ho tro:
- Soan nhap nhat ky.
- Goi y caption ngan gon.
- Canh bao neu anh/noi dung co the lo thong tin rieng tu.

## Cai Dat Va Rieng Tu

Nguoi dung co the:
- Bat/tat thong bao.
- Chon trang thai online/offline.
- An/hien thong tin ca nhan.
- Quan ly du lieu ca nhan.
- Xoa tai khoan.

XPAY AI can ton trong cac tuy chon rieng tu nay khi tra loi.
